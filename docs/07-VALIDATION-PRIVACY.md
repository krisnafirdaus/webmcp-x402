# External validation privacy notice

Effective date: 2026-08-31

Raw-response deletion date: 2026-09-30

This notice applies only to the optional SpendMCP external validation study. It does not turn the testnet demo into a production payment service.

## Controller and contact

Krisna Firdaus, the owner of the `krisnafirdaus/webmcp-x402` repository, controls the study responses. Participants can contact the controller through the GitHub account [`@krisnafirdaus`](https://github.com/krisnafirdaus) or request withdrawal by commenting `withdraw consent` on their validation issue.

## Data collected

Submitting the GitHub issue makes these items public:

- the participant's public GitHub username and profile link;
- the issue timestamp;
- task-completion, elapsed-time, tool-transition, and policy-enforcement answers; and
- free-text explanations and usability feedback.

The study does not ask for names, email addresses, locations, employer details, wallet addresses, private keys, financial information, or other sensitive data. Participants should not include them voluntarily.

## Purpose and legal basis

Responses are processed with the participant's explicit consent to evaluate whether independent users can complete SpendMCP's hackathon demo and understand its human-agent authority boundary. Results may be reported as aggregate counts and medians in the public repository and WebMCP Challenge submission.

De-identified quotations are used only when the participant separately selects the optional quotation permission. A response is still valid when quotation permission is withheld.

## Public visibility and processors

Validation issues are public. GitHub hosts the repository and separately processes account and service data under its [General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Deleting an issue removes the public repository copy, but GitHub may retain service records where its policy or legal obligations permit.

## Retention and withdrawal

Raw validation issues will remain public only through 2026-09-30, covering the announced judging period. The repository owner will then delete them. Aggregate statistics that no longer identify a participant may remain in the repository and submission.

A participant may withdraw before that date without penalty by commenting `withdraw consent` on the issue or contacting [`@krisnafirdaus`](https://github.com/krisnafirdaus). The repository owner will delete the issue, exclude it from future calculations, and remove any quotation attributable to that response. Withdrawal does not invalidate processing completed before the request.

## Age and contribution status

Participation is limited to people aged 18 or older. Study feedback is designated **Not a Contribution** under the repository's Apache-2.0 license. The study consent governs only the evaluation, aggregate reporting, and any separately permitted de-identified quotation; it does not assign ownership of the participant's feedback.
